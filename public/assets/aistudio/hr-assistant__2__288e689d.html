Answer engine (optional)

Without a key, answers are extracted directly from your policy files. With an OpenAI-compatible key, the matching passage is rewritten into a tighter bullet list.

The key is stored only in this browser. Remove it before sharing the device.

How it works

HR uploads company policy documents; everything stays on this device. Each question is matched to the best passages, and only the relevant sentences are shown as bullets. If nothing matches closely enough, it declines instead of guessing.

Privacy

Documents, chat history, branding and settings live in this browser only. The HR PIN is stored as a hash and only gates the settings screens on this device. If an AI key is added, just the matching passages and your question are sent to that API.
